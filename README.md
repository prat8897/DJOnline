# Cue & Phrase

A browser DJ trainer. Two decks, a mixer and a live beat grid, with six guided lessons: decks and faders, cue points, beatmatching, phrasing, the EQ bass swap and filter fades.

**Live:** https://prat8897.github.io/DJOnline/

## Using a MIDI controller

Web MIDI needs Chrome or Edge.

1. Plug in your controller and open the page.
2. Click **Connect MIDI controller** and allow access.
3. Click **Map controls**, click an on-screen control, then move or press the matching control on your hardware. Mappings are saved in the browser. Use **Flip** if a fader or jog wheel runs backwards.

Mouse and keyboard work without a controller (Q/W/A/S for deck A, P/O/K/L for deck B, arrows for the crossfader).

## Tracks

Four built-in synthesized tracks (118–128 BPM) with intro, groove, breakdown and drop sections. **Load file** plays your own audio; BPM and beat position are detected automatically and can be corrected.

Everything is in `index.html`; there is no build step.
